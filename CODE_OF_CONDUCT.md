# Code of conduct

Termwright is built by and for people who keep networks running. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to networking.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Disagree with care and assume good intent.
- Give feedback that helps: what's wrong, why, and what would be better.
- Respect people's time: search existing issues first and give the details needed to help you.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, and sexual content or attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions, or other behaviour a reasonable person would find unwelcome.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through GitHub (the profile of the repository's owner). Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, and warn or block people whose behaviour breaks this code, depending on how serious it is and whether it is repeated.
